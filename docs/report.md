# RainReady2Go Project Report

## Business idea
RainReady2Go is a student venture that sells affordable, ready-to-buy rain gear right on the Cebu Eastern College campus, so students can stay dry the moment the rain starts.

## Problem
- Rain often starts while students are still on campus, and many have no umbrella in their bag.
- Wet clothes, shoes, books and gadgets turn a small shower into a real loss.
- Ordering rain gear online is too slow when the rain is already falling.

## Solution
We keep rain gear where students already are: the school entrance/gate and the canteen. Our website lists the products and prices and lets students place an order, choose a pickup spot, and pay by cash on pickup or GCash.

Products:
| Product | Price |
|---------|-------|
| Pocket Umbrella | ₱95 |
| Disposable Raincoat | ₱65 |
| Backpack Rain Cover | ₱75 |
| Waterproof Shoe Covers | ₱65 |
| Rain Boots | ₱120 |
| RainReady Emergency Kit (bundle) | ₱150 |

## Target customers
Students at Cebu Eastern College who commute to and from school, plus teachers and staff who get caught in sudden rain.

## Survey results
We surveyed 30 students:
- 93% run into rain on their commute at least sometimes.
- 40% chose the pocket umbrella as the product they would most want.
- 70% were interested or very interested in the Emergency Kit.
- 53% said product quality matters most when buying rain gear.
- Most-picked pickup spots: school entrance/gate (43%) and canteen (37%).
