      About us

      Built for the commute students actually have.

      RainReady2Go is a student-focused venture that makes rain protection easy to get when the weather turns. Students are often caught off guard on the way to and from school, and ordering online is too slow to help.

      Our idea is simple: keep affordable, ready-to-buy rain gear where students already are, so staying dry takes a quick purchase instead of a trip across town.

      Created by 3rd Year BSIT students at Cebu Eastern College.
